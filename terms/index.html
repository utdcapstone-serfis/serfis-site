01Scope

These terms cover this site and our internal Distribution Agent pipeline, a university capstone project that quality-checks short-form video content and publishes approved videos to social platforms we operate accounts on. This isn't a public product — it's an internal tool, and these terms exist because some of the platforms we connect to require a published Terms of Service URL.

02What this is

The Distribution Agent is software we built and operate ourselves. It reviews a video against a quality rubric, and once approved, publishes it to one or more social platforms using credentials we hold for our own accounts on those platforms.

03Human approval

No post goes out without a specific human on our team reviewing and approving that exact post for that exact platform first. There is no autonomous or unattended posting, and no setting or flag bypasses this.
