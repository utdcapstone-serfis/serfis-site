Privacy Policy

What we collect through this site and the tools we build on top of it, why we collect it, and how to reach us about it.

Effective — October 1, 2026 · Applies to this site and the Distribution Agent pipeline

01Scope 02What we collect 03How we use it 04Connected platforms 05Sharing 06Retention 07Your rights 08Children 09Changes 10Contact

01Scope

This policy covers this site and the services we operate from it, including our internal Distribution Agent pipeline, which reviews short-form video content for quality and, once approved, publishes it to the social platforms we operate accounts on (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, Bluesky, Pinterest, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, and TikTok). It does not cover the privacy practices of those third-party platforms themselves, which are governed by their own policies.

02What we collect

We keep what we collect deliberately narrow:

  • Contact form submissions. If you write to us through this site, we receive whatever you enter — typically a name, an email address, and a message.
  • Standard server logs. Like any hosted website, our infrastructure records basic technical data for every request — IP address, browser user agent, timestamp, and the page requested — used only for security monitoring and diagnosing outages, never for profiling visitors.
  • Our own social media credentials. To operate our Distribution Agent, we hold OAuth tokens and account identifiers for the social accounts we ourselves own and post from. We do not collect or process data belonging to other platforms' end users through this pipeline.

We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Loading this page's fonts from Google Fonts causes your browser to make a request to Google's servers for those font files, which is the only third-party request this page makes.

03How we use it

Contact form submissions are used only to respond to the person who sent them. Server logs are used only for security and reliability — keeping the site online and catching abuse. Social platform credentials are used only to publish our own already-approved video content and to confirm that a post we made is actually live; every post goes out only after a human on our team has explicitly reviewed and approved that specific post for that specific platform.

04Connected platforms

Our Distribution Agent posts to the following platforms from accounts we control. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles the content and account data once it reaches them:

  • Instagram & Facebook & Threads — Meta Platforms, Inc.
  • YouTube — Google LLC
  • Bluesky — Bluesky Social, PBC
  • Pinterest — Pinterest, Inc.
  • X — X Corp.
  • Reddit — Reddit, Inc.
  • LinkedIn & TikTok — connected via Upload-Post.com on our behalf

We do not use our access to any of these platforms to read, collect, or export data about their other users. Our access is limited to posting our own content and verifying our own posts.

05Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share contact form submissions with anyone outside our team. We share data with a service provider only where that service is the mechanism for the thing you asked us to do — for example, an email submitted through our contact form passing through our email delivery provider so the message reaches us.

06Retention

We keep contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a reasonable record of correspondence, and we delete them on request. Server logs are retained on a short rolling basis for security purposes and then automatically cleared. Social platform credentials are kept only as long as the corresponding account remains part of our active distribution pipeline, and are revoked when a platform is retired from it.

07Your rights

If you've contacted us and want to know what we hold, have it corrected, or have it deleted, email us and we'll handle it directly — we're a small team, so this isn't a self-service portal, it's a real person reading your request.

08Children

This site and our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

09Changes to this policy

This pipeline is under active development. We'll update the effective date above and, where a change is significant, note it on this page as new platforms or data uses are added.
